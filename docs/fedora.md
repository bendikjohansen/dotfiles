# Fedora

## Setup

1. Navigate to the `./fedora.md/` directory.
2. Execute `./setup.sh`

## Philosophy

This build is productive and opinionated. It's stable and isn't in your way. It lives to serve you.

Fedora is the distro of choice because it is the sweet spot between the stable Debian and the bleeding edge Arch. It's stable, yet fairly up to date.
