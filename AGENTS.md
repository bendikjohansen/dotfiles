# dotfiles

A collection of dotfiles to configure personal environments.

## Instructions

- When on macOS, only consider the `./mac` and `./bin` folders unless explicitly
  told otherwise.

## Systems

### MacBook Pro

- *Specifications*: Apple M4, 32 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, macOS Tahoe
- *dotfiles location*: `./mac/`
